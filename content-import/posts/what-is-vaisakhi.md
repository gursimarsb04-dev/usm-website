# What Is Vaisakhi? Meaning and How SSAs Celebrate

Every April, something special happens in cities with big Sikh communities. Streets fill with saffron and blue. Floats roll by with kirtan playing. Volunteers hand out free food to anyone walking past. Kids, grandparents, and college students all show up.

That is Vaisakhi. If you have seen a nagar kirtan parade and wondered what it was all about, here is the guide.

## The short answer

Vaisakhi (also spelled Baisakhi) is one of the most important dates in the Sikh calendar, celebrated on April 13 or 14. It began as a harvest festival in Punjab, and for Sikhs it commemorates 1699, when Guru Gobind Singh, the tenth Guru, founded the Khalsa at Anandpur Sahib. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

## Vaisakhi before 1699: a harvest festival

According to the BBC, Vaisakhi was a long-established harvest festival in Punjab, celebrated well before it took on its added meaning for Sikhs. That is why you may hear Punjabis of different backgrounds talk about Vaisakhi as a harvest celebration too. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

## Why Vaisakhi is important to Sikhs

In 1699, Guru Gobind Singh chose Vaisakhi as the occasion to found the Khalsa in front of thousands at Anandpur Sahib. As the BBC tells it, the Guru asked who was willing to give their life, and five men stepped forward one by one. They became known as the Panj Piare, the "Beloved Five," and were the first initiated into the Khalsa with Amrit. This is the basis of the Sikh initiation ceremony. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

The Khalsa is the community of initiated Sikhs. USM's Sikhi 101 guide explains that Sikhs who take Amrit commit to a code of conduct that includes wearing the Five Ks and keeping daily prayer. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW] Our post on the Khalsa goes deeper.

## Is Vaisakhi the Sikh New Year?

The BBC describes Vaisakhi as the Sikh New Year festival. You may see different calendars and views discussed in Sikh communities, so it is worth asking your local gurdwara how they mark it. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

## How Sikhs celebrate Vaisakhi

- **Gurdwara programs.** Gurdwaras are decorated and visited, with kirtan and prayers. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]
- **Nagar kirtan.** Processions through the streets ("nagar" means town) with kirtan, led by five Sikhs representing the Panj Piare, with Guru Granth Sahib Ji carried in a place of honor. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]
- **Langar and free food** for everyone along the route.
- **Amrit ceremonies.** Many Sikhs choose to be initiated into the Khalsa on Vaisakhi. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]
- **Music and dance**, including bhangra and giddha at cultural events.

## Vaisakhi on campus

Vaisakhi falls during spring semester, which makes it a big moment for Sikh Student Associations. Ideas SSAs use:

- **Vaisakhi kirtan or divaan** on campus or at a local gurdwara [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]
- **Group trip to a nagar kirtan** in a nearby city
- **"What is Vaisakhi?" info table** with chai and a short handout for classmates
- **Turban-tying booth** in saffron and blue
- **Cultural night** with bhangra or giddha teams
- **Seva project** like a food drive, connecting the celebration to service

Plan early. Many campus event bookings and funding requests close weeks before the event.

## A simple Vaisakhi checklist for SSA boards

If you are on an SSA board, here is a planning timeline students have found useful:

1. **Eight weeks out:** pick the event type, book a room, and submit any student government funding request. Deadlines often come earlier than you expect.
2. **Six weeks out:** reach out to a local gurdwara if you want help with kirtan, langar, or speakers. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]
3. **Four weeks out:** design a flyer, post it on Instagram, and share it with partner clubs like the MSA, HSA, or South Asian Student Association.
4. **Two weeks out:** confirm volunteers for setup, food, photos, and cleanup.
5. **Event day:** have a welcome table with a short explainer for first-time guests, and collect emails.
6. **After:** post photos (with permission), thank volunteers, and send a follow-up to everyone who signed in.

## Tips for non-Sikh friends

- "Happy Vaisakhi" is a kind greeting.
- At a nagar kirtan, you are usually welcome to watch and eat. Cover your head near the procession out of respect. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]
- Ask your Sikh friends what Vaisakhi means to them personally.

## FAQ

### What is Vaisakhi?
Vaisakhi is a Sikh festival on April 13 or 14 that commemorates the founding of the Khalsa by Guru Gobind Singh in 1699. It also has roots as a Punjabi harvest festival. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

### Why is Vaisakhi important?
For Sikhs, Vaisakhi marks the creation of the Khalsa and the first Amrit ceremony with the Panj Piare in 1699. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

### How do Sikhs celebrate Vaisakhi?
With gurdwara programs, kirtan, nagar kirtan processions, langar, and for some, taking Amrit. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

### What is a nagar kirtan?
A procession through the streets with hymn singing, led by five Sikhs representing the Panj Piare. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

### Is Vaisakhi the same as Baisakhi?
Yes, Baisakhi is another common spelling.
