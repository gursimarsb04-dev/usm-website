# Sikh Holidays and Gurpurabs: A Student Calendar

You are building your semester calendar. Exams, project deadlines, that one 8 a.m. you will definitely regret. If you are a Sikh student, or a friend, RA, or professor who wants to be thoughtful, it helps to know when the major Sikh observances fall.

Here is a plain guide to Gurpurabs and other key Sikh holidays, and how Sikh Student Associations plan around them.

## The short answer

A Gurpurab is a Sikh celebration connected to the life of a Guru, such as a birth anniversary or martyrdom. The most widely celebrated include Guru Nanak's birthday, Guru Gobind Singh's birthday, the martyrdom of Guru Arjan, and the martyrdom of Guru Tegh Bahadur. Other major Sikh observances include Vaisakhi and Bandi Chhor Divas. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

## What does Gurpurab mean?

"Gurpurab" (also spelled Gurpurb) refers to festivals associated with the lives of the Gurus. The BBC describes them as happy occasions celebrated enthusiastically by Sikhs. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

## Major Gurpurabs

The BBC lists these as the most important Gurpurabs:

| Gurpurab | Usually falls |
|---|---|
| Birthday of Guru Nanak, founder of Sikhi | November (some observe in April) |
| Birthday of Guru Gobind Singh, founder of the Khalsa | January (dates vary) |
| Martyrdom of Guru Arjan | June |
| Martyrdom of Guru Tegh Bahadur | November or December |

[NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW] Dates shift year to year because of the calendar used, and different Sikh organizations publish different calendars, so check with your local gurdwara for exact dates. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

## Other key Sikh observances

- **Vaisakhi** (April 13 or 14): marks the founding of the Khalsa in 1699. See our Vaisakhi guide. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]
- **Bandi Chhor Divas** (same day as Diwali, October or November): marks Guru Hargobind's release from prison with 52 princes. See our Bandi Chhor Divas guide. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

Many Sikh students also observe remembrance days, such as the anniversary of 1984. Our upcoming post on 1984 remembrance on campus covers how SSAs approach that respectfully. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

## How Gurpurabs are celebrated

According to the BBC, common traditions include: [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

- **Akhand Path:** a complete, continuous reading of Guru Granth Sahib Ji, taking about 48 hours and finishing on the day of the Gurpurab
- **Decorated gurdwaras** with flowers, flags, and lights
- **Kirtan**, poems in praise of the Gurus, and talks
- **Processions** led by five Sikhs representing the Panj Piare
- **Karah Parshad** (a sweet blessed food) and **langar** for everyone

## Planning your semester as a Sikh student

- **Check dates in August and January** and add them to your calendar.
- **Email professors early** if an observance conflicts with an exam. Many schools have religious accommodation policies. Ask your dean of students office how yours works.
- **Plan with your SSA.** Fall usually includes Bandi Chhor Divas and Guru Nanak's Gurpurab. Spring usually includes Vaisakhi.
- **Find a gurdwara near campus** if you are far from home.

## Ideas for SSAs

- **Gurpurab kirtan night** on campus [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]
- **Group trip to a local gurdwara** for the program and langar

- **Seva project** on or around the Gurpurab
- **Shared semester calendar** of Sikh observances posted for members and campus staff

## A note on dates and calendars

You may notice that two websites, or two gurdwaras, list different dates for the same Gurpurab. That is normal. Dates are tied to a calendar that does not line up neatly with the one on your phone, and Sikh organizations have discussed different calendar systems over the years. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW] The practical move for students is simple: pick the gurdwara you actually attend, follow its calendar, and share those dates with your SSA and professors.

## How to ask a professor for an accommodation

A short, polite email works best. Something like:

"Hi Professor ___, I am a Sikh student and [date] is [observance], which I observe with my family and community. Would it be possible to [take the quiz on a different day / submit the assignment early]? Thank you for considering it."

Send it early in the term, keep it brief, and follow your school's official process if it has one.

## For RAs, professors, and friends

- Ask Sikh students which observances matter to them. Not everyone marks every day the same way.
- Avoid scheduling required events on major observances when you can.
- A simple "Happy Gurpurab" goes a long way.

## FAQ

### What is a Gurpurab?
A Gurpurab is a Sikh celebration associated with the life of a Guru, such as a birth anniversary or martyrdom. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

### What are the main Sikh holidays?
Major Sikh observances include Vaisakhi, Bandi Chhor Divas, and Gurpurabs such as Guru Nanak's and Guru Gobind Singh's birthdays and the martyrdoms of Guru Arjan and Guru Tegh Bahadur. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

### How do Sikhs celebrate Gurpurab?
With an Akhand Path, kirtan, decorated gurdwaras, processions, Karah Parshad, and langar. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

### When is Guru Nanak's Gurpurab?
It is commonly celebrated in November, with dates that change each year. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]

### Can students get religious accommodations for Sikh holidays?
Many colleges have religious accommodation policies. Check with your dean of students or student affairs office.
