# Scholarships for Sikh Students in the U.S.

College is expensive. Between tuition, books, rent, and that one textbook that costs more than your flight home, it adds up fast. And most general scholarship databases bury the awards built specifically for students like you.

This guide pulls together the scholarships, grants, and interest-free loans created for Sikh students in the United States, plus practical advice for making your application stand out.

**One rule before you start:** amounts, eligibility, and deadlines change every year. Treat this as a map, and always confirm details on each official site before you apply.

## The short answer

Scholarships and aid for Sikh students in the U.S. include the Sikh Education Fund (Association of Sikh Professionals), the 5Rivers Foundation Scholarship, SALDEF scholarships, the Sikh Human Development Foundation's USA program, and the Sikh Scholarship Fund. ## Scholarships and aid for Sikh students

### Sikh Education Fund (Association of Sikh Professionals)
Since 1989, the Sikh Education Fund, a project of the Association of Sikh Professionals, has helped Sikh students at U.S. colleges and universities with scholarships and interest-free loans. Loans are repaid after graduation, and the repaid money supports the next students. [VERIFY]

**Good fit if:** you are at a U.S. college and could use a grant or an interest-free loan.

### 5Rivers Foundation Scholarship
The 5Rivers Foundation has offered scholarship grants of up to $5,000 to Sikh students attending or planning to attend an accredited U.S. undergraduate or graduate program. Published criteria have included a minimum 2.5 GPA and a family income cap. Awards are not renewable, but past recipients can reapply. [VERIFY]

**Good fit if:** you have financial need and are enrolled or about to enroll in a U.S. program.

### SALDEF scholarships
SALDEF, the Sikh American Legal Defense and Education Fund, has offered scholarships to Sikh American students with academic merit and financial need. [VERIFY]

**Good fit if:** you have a strong academic record and a clear story about your field.

### Sikh Human Development Foundation (USA)
Best known for scholarships in Punjab, the foundation also lists a USA scholarship program. Check its page for current eligibility. [VERIFY]

### Sikh Scholarship Fund
Supports Sikh students who show academic excellence, leadership potential, and commitment to community service. [VERIFY]

## Money for your SSA, too

If you are on a Sikh Student Association board, there is funding for your chapter as well. The Sikh Coalition has run a Sangat Seva Grant for SSAs, supporting programming like Sikh Awareness Week, leadership workshops, and seva projects. Watch for the next round. [VERIFY]

And if your school does not have an SSA yet, USM's starter kit includes a funding guide.

## How to write a stronger application

Sikh-specific scholarships are often read by people who care about the community. That changes what "strong" looks like.

1. **Be specific about seva.** "I volunteer at the gurdwara" is common. What did you actually do, for how long, and what changed because of it?
2. **Connect your field to your values.** Why law, medicine, engineering, or teaching? What does Sikhi have to do with it? Reviewers want to see you have thought about it. [NEEDS GURSIMAR REVIEW]
3. **Show leadership, not titles.** Being SSA president matters less than what your SSA did while you led it.
4. **Ask for recommendations early.** Give recommenders at least three weeks and a short summary of what you would like them to mention.
5. **Reuse, then tailor.** Write one strong core essay about your story, then adapt the opening and closing for each award.
6. **Apply to more than one.** Individual awards can be small. Several together can change a year.

## A simple scholarship tracker

Make a spreadsheet with these columns: award name, link, amount, deadline, eligibility, documents needed, recommenders, status. Check it every Sunday. It sounds boring. It works.
